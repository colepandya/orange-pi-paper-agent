# Build 42  (strategy S7): the reasoning engine, audited and rebuilt
Built from an independent review of build 41. Each point was checked against the code before acting; several were already true or already done, and some were wrong (see docs/REASONING_ENGINE.md).
* **Deterministic only, with INFLUENCE modes.** The LLM is no longer reachable from the pipeline or the trader at all (its files remain, unused). A new setting says how much the reasoning engine may affect trading: `off`, `observe` (records everything including what it WOULD have blocked, changes nothing), `guard` (the default: it may veto factually broken ideas and make a trade smaller, never create or enlarge one) and `active` (full influence). The old `engine` setting is gone.
* **Red team v2 (skeptic.py).** Every objection is tagged with the RULE that raised it and graded: info < confidence < size < hold < veto. Only a short list of hard-veto rules (denied, stale, wrong company, duplicate story, factually contradicted or superseded) may veto in guard mode. Every review also records what `active` would have done, and a counterfactual SCORECARD follows what happened to the ideas it would have blocked, per rule, with honest intervals: rules earn influence from evidence.
* **Contradictions must ALIGN** (same company, same kind of news, overlapping time, opposite implication, not a re-run of the same story, not already in the news the idea was built on). Tested against a labelled corpus with precision and recall. (In build 41, 44 of 84 ideas were flagged; most of that was noise.)
* **A cached review is dropped when new news about the company arrives** (it was reused for 12 hours regardless).
* **Canonical reasoning record** (`reasoning_events`): event state (rumoured/proposed/agreed/pending/approved/completed/rejected/denied), novelty (new/update/recap/correction), materiality, independent-source count (five sites repeating one wire story are one source), horizon profile, and the confidence split into separate components (entity, classification, truth, role, causal, direction probability, magnitude, timing, completeness). Every analysis is logged, including the ones it abstained on, with the reason. A denial or correction now REPLACES earlier evidence.
* **Graph v2**: statistical co-movement links need both 60- and 120-session agreement AND must survive removing the market's influence, are labelled "not a cause", and never create trades (graph.trade_on_correlation, off). A rival moves WITH industry-wide news and against company-specific news. Every derived idea records its mechanism and sign confidence. Relationships carry provenance and validity dates; expired or retired ones are ignored. A third hop is explained but never scored.
* **Macro v2** (config/macro_exposures.json): drivers have CHANNELS (sign, confidence, lag, horizon); opposing channels are netted and a cancelling group is reported as mixed instead of guessed.
* **SEC facts replace the LLM filings reader** (facts.py): revenue, margins, cash and debt, share dilution and cash burn from SEC's free structured data; the red team and `ask why` use them.
* **8-K reading extended**: cyber incidents, dilution, auditor change, restructuring, late-filing notices, offerings, activist stakes. Routine items stay neutral.
* **Multi-horizon outcomes** (pathways.py): returns at 1/3/5/10/20 sessions, best/worst excursion, target-or-stop first, gap, versus the market and the sector; hierarchical statistics that shrink small groups toward their parent. `python3 -m paper_agent scorecard`; Intelligence tab -> Reasoning scorecard.
* **Entity bug**: a Techmeme-style byline ("Simon Sharwood / The Register") made Google articles match Simon Property Group. Fixed (bylines stripped, first-name-plus-surname is a person).
* Two collisions found by the tests and fixed: a variable named `mode` shadowed the cycle mode and silently disabled the learning, missed-trade and gaps stages; a second GIVEN_NAMES definition overwrote the first.
